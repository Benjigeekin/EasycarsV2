Rental terms
 

Rental terms

DRIVER & LICENCE Requirements
To rent a vehicle, the renter must have the following:

- A valid driving license of category B.
- If the driving license was issued outside the European Union, an International Driving Permit (IDP) may be required.
- A passport or an ID card (for citizens of the European Union).
- A bank card in the renter’s name, valid for at least three months.

If the renter provides expired or damaged documents, or if the company’s staff have reasonable grounds to suspect that the documents are fraudulent, the rental provider reserves the right to refuse the rental service.

Additional requirements:
- Minimum driver age: 22 years.
- Minimum driving experience: 3 years.
Payment for rental
Payment: Only via bank transfer (Pre-pay) or credit / debit card (at time of car pick up)
bank card only: VISA / Vpay / MasterCard / Maestro / Amex / Union Pay / JCB
Make payment by cash isn't possible.
Deposit
- Bank card requirement: The card needs to be a valid minimum of 3 months. The bank card should contain. Number / valid date / holder name and surname.
- We accept CREDIT or DEBIT cards
- Accepted cards VISA / MasterCard / AMEX / Vpay / JCB / Union Pay / Bancontact
IMPORTANT: Lessee can't leave a deposit by MAESTRO card.
- We will refund the deposit 5-10 calendars days, after rental.
IMPORTANT - You can't leave the deposit in cash.
IMPORTANT - You can't leave the deposit if your car is issued by PaySera / N26 / Revolut and banks that issued cards via mobile application.
CDW and DAMAGE EXCESS
In the case, that the car's bodywork gets damaged during the rental, you’re covered by the Collision Damage Waiver (CDW). This means that the most you'll pay towards repairs are the damage excess. Amount of (form 500,00 EUR to 2000,00 EUR) CDW is depended on car classes / your age / driving experience. Amount of CDW you can clarify at car reservation moment, or at car pick-up time.
- This cover is only valid if you stick to the terms of the rental agreement.
- It doesn't cover other parts of the car like: (undercarriage / wheels / interior / windows) or charges (e.g. for car towing or for the time when the car was in the service station, which led to the impossibility of renting it out to other clients), or anything in the car like your personal belongings or child seats.
- If the car was destroyed in the accident or was stolen, CDW will be 20% of the car value
- You can add for the additional price the "Super Insurance" with full protection. In this case, CDW is 0,00 EUR. price for "Super Insurance" depended on car classes / your age / driving experience. You can clarify the price of "Super insurance" at the car reservation moment, or at car pick-up time.
Mileage limit
- You have a mileage limit of 250 km/day (consider all rental periods e.g. if you rent a car for 10 days, you can drive 2500 km)
For an extra price, you can purchase an unlimited mileage option. The price for unlimited mileage, you can clarify at car reservation moment
Trip to baltic states. Estonia or Lithuania.
- For extra 40,00 EUR, you can visit Estonia or Lithuania.
(one-time payment)
Trip to other EU countries.
- Going with our cars out of Latvia is allowed but by agreement.
- If you want to visit other EU countries, please while making a reservation match that option and specify in the comments which countries you want to visit, and how many kilometres you are planning to overcome. And our manager will send you an offer for such rent.
- If the Lessee does not notify the Lessor about travelling outside of the territory of Latvia in a timely way and drives the car outside of the territory of Latvia without permission, then the following penalties (penalties amount will be showed in rental contract)
- Trip out of the EU isn't allowed.
Fuel policy.
Full to full or Same to Same.
You will receive the car with the full or partly full tank. Before the car returns, please replace the fuel you’ve used to the same level as you receive the car.
- If you forgot to refuel the car, then the amount of missing fuel will be charged from your deposit. price: 5,50EUR = each missing fuel liter.
- At car pick up time, you can add the extra option "FULL to EMPTY" for 80.00 EUR. That's mean that you will take the car with a full tank and can return with an empty tank
Car wash and cleaning after rental.
- After the rental, you don't need to clean the interior or wash the car body.
- For non-clean cars no extra payments.
- - The only exception is those cases when it is necessary to do dry cleaning of the interior.
- The price depends on the degree of contamination of the cabin.
- Dry cleaning is necessary if: There are stains on the seats / carpets / and other interior surfaces that cannot be removed without dry cleaning.
The price includes.
- KASKO and OCTA insurance (With CDW)
- Child seats (if you need them)
- Second driver
- Km limit 250 km per day (consider of all rental period)
- Navigation systems
- Discount cards on gasoline stations
- Free delivery on any place in Riga / Jurmala / airport (if the rental period is 3 or more days)
- Free transfer after rental from our office to the airport or to your home.
- Help on the road 24/7
- Winter tyers (when is snowy)
For an extra price, you can get
- Unlimited mileage
- Full insurance CDW and DAMAGE EXCESS is 0,00 EUR
- Car delivery to any location
- Transfer to any place
- Fuel policy "FULL TO EMPTY"
- Private driver
- If need something special, just contact us, we will be happy to help you.
